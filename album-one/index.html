The Story

Wyatt Croft is a country project out of Lexington, Kentucky: horse farms off Paris Pike, black plank fences, tobacco barns with one light left on, and the KY-1914 two-lane through Amish country down by Crofton. The videos stay true to that world, with horses doing horse things, buggies rolling away down the road, and riders seen from behind.

Made with AI. Wyatt Croft's songs, vocals and videos are created with AI tools (music generated with Suno; visuals with AI image and video models). Wyatt Croft is a fictional artist persona, and the people on screen are not real individuals.
